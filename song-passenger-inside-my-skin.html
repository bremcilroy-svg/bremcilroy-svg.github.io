Passenger Inside My Skin

Lyrics written by Bre McIlroy

Music and vocals created with AI

Living with an unpredictable body while holding on to possibility.

Invisible illness, resilience

Spotify artist profile

Verse 1

Wake up slow, the ceiling spins, A battle starts before it begins. The air feels heavy, walls lean low, I’m learning to move when my body says “no.”

Verse 2

Heartbeat skips, it races, then falls, I steady myself on the nearest wall. I’ve learned to smile through the blur, So no one asks what’s wrong with her.

Pre-Chorus

The world looks steady, but I sway, Inside my frame, I drift away. A secret fight I never chose, A fragile dance that no one knows.

Chorus

Every step’s a fragile deal, Balancing on what I feel. The world tilts left, it tilts again, I’m a passenger inside my skin.

Verse 3

Crowds rush past, I move too slow, I’m pacing my breath, but they don’t know. It’s hard to trust the ground beneath, When it slips away like a thief.

Verse 4

Plans get lost to dizzy days, My body writes rules in twisted ways. I dream of running, free, unbound, But for now, I fight to stand my ground.

Pre-Chorus

They see me walking, think I’m fine, But balance breaks on a fragile line. This storm inside, it pulls, it bends, It never stops, it never ends.

Chorus

Every step’s a fragile deal, Balancing on what I feel. The world tilts left, it tilts again, I’m a passenger inside my skin.

Bridge 1

I count the breaths, I count the beats, I hold on tight, I won’t concede. Even when the world’s a blur, I remind myself—I still endure.

Verse 5

Doctors speak in careful tones, A language carved from charts and bones. But only I can feel the fight, Each flicker of dark, each flash of light.

Bridge 2

If strength’s a mountain, I’ll still climb, Even if it takes a lifetime. This broken compass I’ve been given, Won’t keep me from the life I’m living.

Chorus

Every step’s a fragile deal, Balancing on what I feel. The world tilts left, it tilts again, I’m a passenger inside my skin.

Final Chorus (softer, then rising)

Every step’s a fragile deal, Balancing on what I feel. The world tilts left, it tilts again, I’m a passenger inside my skin. And though my body pulls me down, My spirit rises, safe and sound.

Outro

So here I stand, though walls may spin, I’ll learn to dance with what I’m in. The world tilts left, it tilts again… But I’m still here, I still begin.
